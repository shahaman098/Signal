# North Star

Signal exists to reduce creative fatigue and shorten the path from portfolio review to the next testable brief.

## Product Rules

- Every recommendation must be grounded in live owned and competitor creative data.
- Qwen output is advisory until a human operator approves the brief.
- The frontend must stay free of hardcoded fake production results.
- The deployment path must remain simple enough to prove on Alibaba Cloud during the hackathon.
